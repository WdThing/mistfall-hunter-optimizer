Optimization Mode

Lowest Rarity Stat First

Stat First: (1) generates affix combinations at or below 110% of the Reference Cost using the price-by-total-level lookup, (2) finds real equipment for each, and (3) follows the stat-versus-average-price Pareto frontier. Lower-priced validated results remain eligible; only more expensive results are filtered when a cheaper result exists.
